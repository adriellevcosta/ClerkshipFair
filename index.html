YOUR CORE SITE, YOUR NEXT CHAPTER

Why Decatur?

Within miles of big-city conveniences, the Decatur Core Site offers exceptional clerkships and individualized attention. Our preceptors and hospital staff are committed to creating a welcoming environment that supports every student's growth. Since 2009, the site has hosted ACOM and AMEC medical students.

✦ 1:1 preceptor-to-student ratio
✦ Hands-on procedures and surgical opportunities
✦ Robotic and specialty surgery exposure
✦ Almost all core clerkships available locally
✦ 24-hour student building access
✦ Free meals and parking
✦ Affordable housing options
✦ Welcoming community and passionate preceptors
YOUR CLINICAL FIELD GUIDE

Core Clerkships

Core experiences available at the Decatur site include:

  • Inpatient and outpatient Family Medicine
  • Internal Medicine
  • Surgery
  • Osteopathic Obstetrics and Pediatrics
  • Osteopathic Orthopedics
  • Comprehensive inpatient Psychiatry, including a separate children's unit

Specialty clerkships: Cardiology, Infectious Disease, Urology, PM&R, Anesthesia, Endocrinology, Pathology, and more.

Come experience the difference.

The Decatur Core Site prides itself on individualized student attention, with a variety of preceptors and specialty experiences that can be tailored to student interests. Students consistently tell us there is something special about the Decatur Core Site. We invite you to experience it for yourself.

Two da Vinci robots support general, urologic, and gynecologic surgery. The Mako system supports robotic-assisted orthopedic surgery.

DECATUR MORGAN HOSPITAL

Hospital
at a glance.

Decatur Morgan Hospital is an affiliate of the Huntsville Hospital System and serves Decatur, Hartselle, and surrounding communities in Morgan County.

Contact Dr. Patrick →
What makes up Decatur Morgan Hospital?+

Five campuses include two acute-care facilities, two emergency departments, a freestanding 64-bed behavioral health inpatient facility, and a freestanding Rehab/Wellness Center.

What services and facilities are available?+

Services include an expanded Emergency Department staffed by board-certified Emergency Medicine physicians, an expanded Labor & Delivery unit with board-certified OB/GYN physicians, and a state-of-the-art vascular surgery operating room with a board-certified vascular surgeon.

What is the medical staff like?+

The medical staff includes more than 225 physicians representing a wide variety of medical specialties.

Are there community and volunteer opportunities?+

Yes. Opportunities include a full-time ambulance service for Morgan County and a Mobile Medical unit supporting volunteer health screenings, education, and disaster relief, along with other hospital and community volunteer opportunities.
